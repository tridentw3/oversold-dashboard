Methodology & data audit

Combined ranking

  • Upside rank: Tier-1 names (profitable 2027E and 2028E, not ANOMALY) ordered by upside to the end-2027 target. Target = 2028E EPS × exit P/E; exit P/E = min(g, 30x) if g ≥ 30% else max(0.8 g, 10x), ×0.8 haircut when a loss-of-exclusivity / direct competitor lands inside ~2 yrs. Bear = (IBES 2028E − 1 SD) × trough P/E; demoted (still ranked) if bear < −30%.
  • Oversold rank: order by oversold flags (/10), then average indicator percentile, then turn signals — identical to the Oversold ranking tab.
  • Combined # = sum of the two ranks, lowest first. Only names rankable on both dimensions get a combined number.
  • ANOMALY (carried over from the original runs): exit multiple ≥2× the name's 5-yr forward P/E, 2–3 broker consensus with wide dispersion, or commodity / FX / cyclical EPS deck — the 30x-cap / 10x-floor rule mechanically inflates the target, so these are shown but excluded from the upside rank.
  • Prices: every ticker re-priced with the latest Yahoo quote; consensus estimates remain as of the report dates (Oct 22 – Nov 6, 2026). Stock splits after the report date are adjusted (SNDK 8:1); HK-line reports (BABA, TCOM) keep their report price because EPS is in local currency.
  • AI chip / infrastructure filter: semis & semi-cap, memory, AI servers / networking / optics, data-centre power, cooling & REITs, neoclouds / miners-turned-hosts. Hyperscalers and software users of AI are not tagged. Toggling re-ranks the remaining names.
